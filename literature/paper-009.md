# paper-009 — MAPTRx target engagement

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Tau-targeting antisense oligonucleotide MAPT<sub>Rx</sub> in mild Alzheimer's disease: a phase 1b, randomized, placebo-controlled trial. Mummery CJ et al. Nat Med (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37095250/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02326-3) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/37095250)

**Finding:** In 46 participants, intrathecal MAPTRx produced dose-dependent CSF total-Tau reductions; safety was the primary endpoint.

**Limitation:** The small early-phase trial was not designed to establish clinical efficacy; its findings do not transfer to oral small molecules.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Separate target engagement, route-specific exposure, safety, and functional outcomes.
