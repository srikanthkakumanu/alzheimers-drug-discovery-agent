# paper-010 — Semorinemab: Tauriel

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Safety and Efficacy of Semorinemab in Individuals With Prodromal to Mild Alzheimer Disease: A Randomized Clinical Trial. Teng E et al. JAMA Neurol (2022).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35696185/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2022.1375) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/35696185)

**Finding:** Semorinemab did not slow clinical progression versus placebo over the blinded trial in prodromal-to-mild Alzheimer disease.

**Limitation:** Tests one antibody approach, patient population, and stage; it does not disprove every Tau mechanism.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Include negative clinical evidence when evaluating Tau-first confidence.
