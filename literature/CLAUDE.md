# Literature records — rules (auto-loaded in `literature/`)

Real publications summarized at **abstract level**; background only, never evidence about a synthetic candidate.
- A summary is one finding plus one limitation, not a methods appraisal; say so when a decision depends on methods, subgroups, or adverse events.
- A trial report describes that study, not a drug’s current approval or pipeline status.
- **Adding a paper:** verify PMID and DOI in PubMed or Europe PMC (never from memory); required fields are in [data contracts](../docs/architecture/data-contracts.md); keep PMIDs unique; add an index row. Web-search results (e.g. Tavily) are leads only, never a citation source.
- Summarize in your own words; do not paste abstracts or full text.
