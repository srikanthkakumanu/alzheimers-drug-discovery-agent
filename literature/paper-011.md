# paper-011 — Semorinemab: Lauriet

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Randomized Phase II Study of the Safety and Efficacy of Semorinemab in Participants With Mild-to-Moderate Alzheimer Disease: Lauriet. Monteiro C et al. Neurology (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37643887/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000207663) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/37643887)

**Finding:** A cognitive coprimary endpoint favored treatment, but the functional coprimary endpoint and global measures did not; the prespecified study outcome was negative.

**Limitation:** An isolated positive endpoint should not be presented as established overall efficacy.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Preserve endpoint hierarchy and report discordant outcomes together.
