# paper-006 — Tau uptake through HSPGs

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Heparan sulfate proteoglycans mediate internalization and propagation of specific proteopathic seeds. Holmes BB et al. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (2013).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23898162/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1301440110) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/23898162)

**Finding:** Interfering with heparan sulfate proteoglycans reduced Tau fibril uptake and propagation in experimental systems.

**Limitation:** Uptake mechanisms can depend on seed preparation and model; blocking uptake is not demonstrated clinical benefit.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Distinguish reduced entry from direct inhibition of Tau aggregation in cell assays.
