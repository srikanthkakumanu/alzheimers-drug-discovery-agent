# paper-015 — Lecanemab: Clarity AD

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Lecanemab in Early Alzheimer's Disease. van Dyck CH et al. N Engl J Med (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36449413/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2212948) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/36449413)

**Finding:** Lecanemab produced less decline than placebo on cognition/function at 18 months and reduced amyloid markers, with adverse events.

**Limitation:** Treatment effect is not reversal or cure; selected population, follow-up, and safety constrain interpretation.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Use as amyloid-pathway comparator evidence without extrapolating to portfolio efficacy.
