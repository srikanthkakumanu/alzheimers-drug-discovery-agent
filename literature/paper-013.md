# paper-013 — Tilavonemab trial

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Tilavonemab in early Alzheimer's disease: results from a phase 2, randomized, double-blind study. Florian H et al. Brain (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36730056/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awad024) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/36730056)

**Finding:** Tilavonemab did not significantly improve the primary CDR-SB outcome versus placebo at 96 weeks in early Alzheimer disease.

**Limitation:** Negative results are specific to the tested antibody and setting; they still raise the evidence burden for Tau programs.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Make mechanism differences explicit rather than ignoring negative class evidence.
