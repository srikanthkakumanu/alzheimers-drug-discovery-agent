# paper-002 — Biological research framework

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). NIA-AA Research Framework: Toward a biological definition of Alzheimer's disease. Jack CR et al. Alzheimers Dement (2018).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29653606/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalz.2018.02.018) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/29653606)

**Finding:** Organizes research biomarkers into amyloid, pathological Tau, and neurodegeneration categories and separates biology from symptoms.

**Limitation:** The 2018 framework was intended for research, not routine clinical care; later criteria update it.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Keep biomarker and functional outcomes distinct in review packets.
