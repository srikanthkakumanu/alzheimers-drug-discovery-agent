# paper-012 — Gosuranemab: TANGO

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). TANGO: a placebo-controlled randomized phase 2 study of efficacy and safety of the anti-tau monoclonal antibody gosuranemab in early Alzheimer's disease. Shulman M et al. Nat Aging (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38012285/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00523-w) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/38012285)

**Finding:** Treatment lowered unbound N-terminal Tau in CSF without a favorable clinical effect on the reported cognitive/functional assessment.

**Limitation:** One epitope and antibody modality; biomarker engagement is not a validated surrogate for benefit.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Use as a counterexample to promoting on target engagement alone.
