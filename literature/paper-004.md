# paper-004 — Patient-derived Tau structure

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Cryo-EM structures of tau filaments from Alzheimer's disease. Fitzpatrick AWP et al. Nature (2017).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28678775/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature23002) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/28678775)

**Finding:** Cryo-EM resolved paired helical and straight Tau filament structures from Alzheimer brain material.

**Limitation:** Structural observations from limited source material do not establish druggability or predict efficacy.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Ask whether a test system represents relevant Tau conformations; docking alone cannot validate a candidate.
