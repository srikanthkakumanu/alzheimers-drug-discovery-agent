# paper-014 — Donanemab: TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Donanemab in Early Symptomatic Alzheimer Disease: The TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 Randomized Clinical Trial. Sims JR et al. JAMA (2023).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37459141/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.13239) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/37459141)

**Finding:** Donanemab slowed clinical progression in the studied early symptomatic population with amyloid and Tau pathology.

**Limitation:** Benefits and adverse events must be interpreted together; this is an amyloid-directed antibody, not evidence for a Tau small molecule.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Provides comparator trial design and patient-stratification context, not a cross-trial superiority claim.
