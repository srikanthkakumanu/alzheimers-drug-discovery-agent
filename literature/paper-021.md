# paper-021 — LMTM phase 3, mild-to-moderate AD

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09 via Europe PMC). Efficacy and safety of tau-aggregation inhibitor therapy in patients with mild or moderate Alzheimer's disease: a randomised, controlled, double-blind, parallel-arm, phase 3 trial. Gauthier S et al. Lancet (2016).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27863809/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736(16)31275-2) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/27863809)

**Finding:** The tau-aggregation inhibitor LMTM at 75 mg or 125 mg twice daily showed no benefit over control on the coprimary ADAS-Cog and ADCS-ADL endpoints at 65 weeks; the authors concluded the results do not suggest benefit as add-on treatment.

**Limitation:** Specific to one compound, dose range, and mild-to-moderate population; it does not test other aggregation-inhibitor chemotypes or earlier disease stages.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Most directly relevant counterevidence for this portfolio: a small-molecule Tau aggregation inhibitor failed its primary analyses in phase 3.
