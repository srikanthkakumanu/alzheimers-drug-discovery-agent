# paper-017 — NLRP3 mechanism

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). NLRP3 is activated in Alzheimer's disease and contributes to pathology in APP/PS1 mice. Heneka MT et al. Nature (2013).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23254930/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11729) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/23254930)

**Finding:** Inflammasome activation was observed in human disease tissue; NLRP3/caspase-1 deficiency reduced pathology-related outcomes in APP/PS1 mice.

**Limitation:** Genetic deficiency and a familial-amyloid mouse model do not establish the effects of a chronic human inhibitor.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Supports exploratory inflammatory comparators with model and mechanism caveats.
