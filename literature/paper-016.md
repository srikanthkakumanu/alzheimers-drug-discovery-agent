# paper-016 — TREM2 genetic association

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Variant of TREM2 associated with the risk of Alzheimer's disease. Jonsson T et al. N Engl J Med (2013).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23150908/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa1211103) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/23150908)

**Finding:** A rare TREM2 variant was associated with increased Alzheimer disease risk across discovery and replication samples.

**Limitation:** Association does not determine the direction, timing, or safety of a therapeutic intervention.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Maintain microglial biology as an alternative hypothesis; do not infer efficacy from genetic association.
