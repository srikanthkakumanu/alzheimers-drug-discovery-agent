# paper-005 — Experimental Tau propagation

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Transmission and spreading of tauopathy in transgenic mouse brain. Clavaguera F et al. Nat Cell Biol (2009).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19503072/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1901) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/19503072)

**Finding:** Brain extracts containing pathological Tau induced filament formation and spread in Tau-expressing recipient mice.

**Limitation:** Experimental inoculation and transgenic expression differ from sporadic human disease.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Motivates seeding assays while requiring explicit model limitations.
