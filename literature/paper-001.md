# paper-001 — Tau staging

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Neuropathological stageing of Alzheimer-related changes. Braak H et al. Acta Neuropathol (1991).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1759558/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf00308809) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/1759558)

**Finding:** An autopsy series described six stages based on the anatomical distribution of neurofibrillary changes.

**Limitation:** Cross-sectional pathology cannot establish therapeutic causality or a treatment effect.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Use stage-aware hypotheses; do not equate Tau burden with a proven intervention target.
