# paper-008 — Preclinical Tau lowering

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Tau reduction prevents neuronal loss and reverses pathological tau deposition and seeding in mice with tauopathy. DeVos SL et al. Sci Transl Med (2017).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28123067/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aag0481) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/28123067)

**Finding:** Tau-directed ASOs reduced Tau and improved pathological outcomes in a mutant-Tau mouse model; primate work showed distribution and lowering.

**Limitation:** A transgenic tauopathy model and an ASO mechanism cannot validate an unrelated small molecule in Alzheimer disease.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Use as mechanistic rationale for lowering, with modality and model boundaries stated.
