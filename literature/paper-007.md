# paper-007 — Tau conformational diversity

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Distinct tau prion strains propagate in cells and mice and define different tauopathies. Sanders DW et al. Neuron (2014).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24857020/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.04.047) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/24857020)

**Finding:** Distinct Tau conformations propagated characteristic patterns in experimental systems, with differences across tauopathies.

**Limitation:** Engineered systems and selected patient material do not establish universal strain behavior or drug response.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Require more than one seed preparation before generalizing activity.
