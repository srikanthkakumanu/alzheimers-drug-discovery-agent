# paper-003 — Updated diagnostic principles

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09). Revised criteria for diagnosis and staging of Alzheimer's disease: Alzheimer's Association Workgroup. Jack CR et al. Alzheimers Dement (2024).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38934362/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.13859) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/38934362)

**Finding:** Updates the biological definition and staging approach, distinguishing earlier Core 1 from later Core 2 biomarkers.

**Limitation:** General diagnostic principles are not a step-by-step workflow, treatment protocol, or proof that a biomarker is a drug-development surrogate.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Version diagnostic assumptions; avoid using a p-Tau change alone as an efficacy claim.
