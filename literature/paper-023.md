# paper-023 — Unbound brain exposure (Kp,uu)

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09 via Europe PMC). On the rate and extent of drug delivery to the brain. Hammarlund-Udenaes M et al. Pharm Res (2008).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18058202/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11095-007-9502-2) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/18058202)

**Finding:** Describes brain drug delivery with three parameters: Kp,uu (unbound brain-to-blood ratio), CLin, and Vu,brain. Kp,uu varies about 150-fold across CNS-active drugs, compared with about 2,000-fold for total-concentration log BB.

**Limitation:** Conceptual review; it supplies no values for any candidate and does not prescribe a study design.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Defines why the `cns_exposure_engagement` gate needs measured unbound exposure, not permeability or total brain concentration.
