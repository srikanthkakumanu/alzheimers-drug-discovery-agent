# paper-022 — LMTM phase 3, mild AD (revised analysis)

**PUBLISHED EVIDENCE — abstract-level review** (verified 2026-11-09 via Europe PMC). Potential of Low Dose Leuco-Methylthioninium Bis(Hydromethanesulphonate) (LMTM) Monotherapy for Treatment of Mild Alzheimer's Disease: Cohort Analysis as Modified Primary Outcome in a Phase III Clinical Trial. Wilcock GK et al. J Alzheimers Dis (2018).
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29154277/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3233/jad-170560) · [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/29154277)

**Finding:** In an 18-month phase 3 trial in mild AD (100 mg vs 4 mg LMTM twice daily), the originally designed primary endpoint was not met; cohort comparisons defined in a statistical analysis plan revised before unblinding were reported as favorable.

**Limitation:** Revised-plan cohort comparisons are not the original randomized comparison; the authors state a further suitably randomized trial is required.

**Use in NeuroQuest:** Example of endpoint-hierarchy discipline: report the failed prespecified primary result before any reanalysis.
