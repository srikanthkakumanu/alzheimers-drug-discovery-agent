## Positioning
NeuroQuest does not design molecules or predict efficacy. Its proposed contribution is narrower: organizing evidence, exposing uncertainty, and keeping decisions traceable and human-owned. No benchmark, cost advantage, partnership, or platform equivalence is claimed.

For therapeutic comparators use the [literature index](../literature/README.md) (published Tau and amyloid trials). Check trial registries and official labels before stating any pipeline status.
