# Alzheimer’s pathways and evidence boundaries

Published background, verified 2026-11-09; applying it to the portfolio is a demonstration hypothesis. Pathways interact; this is a research map, not clinical guidance.

| Pathway | Evidence | Why it matters here | Main uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tau aggregation / seeding | [structure](../literature/paper-004.md), [propagation](../literature/paper-005.md), [strains](../literature/paper-007.md), [uptake](../literature/paper-006.md) | Mechanism question for the fictional portfolio | Model effects may not transfer to human disease |
| Tau lowering | [ASO preclinical](../literature/paper-008.md), [phase 1b](../literature/paper-009.md) | Separates lowering from aggregation inhibition | Engagement is not established clinical efficacy |
| Amyloid | [donanemab](../literature/paper-014.md), [lecanemab](../literature/paper-015.md) | Comparator clinical evidence | Benefit, adverse events, and eligibility must be read together |
| Microglia / inflammation | [TREM2](../literature/paper-016.md), [NLRP3](../literature/paper-017.md) | Keeps alternative hypotheses open | Genetics or mouse effects do not establish drug benefit |
| Biomarkers / copathology | [2018 framework](../literature/paper-002.md), [2024 criteria](../literature/paper-003.md), [neuropathology](../literature/paper-020.md) | Separates stage, pathology, and outcomes | Biomarkers are not interchangeable endpoints |

## Questions to ask before accepting a claim
1. Does the evidence measure Tau amount, conformation, uptake, seeding, or neuronal function?
2. Does the candidate act on that process specifically?
3. Are free exposure and assay validity known?
4. Is a biomarker change being mistaken for patient benefit?

Tau counterevidence ([Tauriel](../literature/paper-010.md), [Lauriet](../literature/paper-011.md), [TANGO](../literature/paper-012.md), [tilavonemab](../literature/paper-013.md)) must stay visible, without assuming all Tau modalities are equivalent.
