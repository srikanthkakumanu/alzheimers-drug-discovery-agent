# ADR-006 — Human-controlled advancement with independent evidence gates

**Status: proposed** — awaiting owner review · type: `decision-governance`

## Context
A composite score can conceal a decisive artifact, hazard, or replication failure. A research recommendation is different from authorization to run a study or enter clinical development.

## Decision
Require the independent evidence gates described in the scoring contract plus a recorded human decision. Failed gates block; unresolved evidence prevents advancement. A recommendation may request follow-up without approving promotion or study execution.

## Alternatives
| Option | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Score-only nomination | Easy to automate, but masks fatal evidence gaps. |
| LLM approval | Fast, but conflates generated judgment with accountable scientific authorization. |

## Consequences
Advancement remains traceable and conservative about unknowns. Human review is a deliberate dependency; approval records and gate definitions must eventually be implemented and tested.

## Validation
Review C/D/N blocked cases and F/O missing-evidence cases. Existing tests exercise demonstration rules, not regulator acceptance or genuine study qualification.

## Revisit
Tailor gate requirements to a reviewed development stage before any real program uses them; document supersession rather than rewriting a prior approval.
