# ADR-009 — Versioned regulatory references and bounded interpretation

**Status: proposed** — awaiting owner review · type: `evidence-governance`

## Context
Regulatory guidance, draft guidance, scientific diagnostic criteria, and trial reports serve different purposes. An undated summary can hide changes or overstate applicability.

## Decision
Record source status, version/date, verification date, and access scope. Distinguish a published scientific observation from a requirement or authorization. Recheck official sources before consequential development decisions.

## Alternatives
| Option | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Undated general checklist | Short, but cannot show currency or stage applicability. |
| Treat biomarker literature as approval policy | Convenient, but confuses scientific evidence with regulatory judgment. |

## Consequences
A reviewer can inspect applicability and uncertainty. Source verification requires human effort, and summaries remain incomplete substitutes for stage-specific expert advice.

## Validation
Check official source links and draft/final labels. Do not infer clinical benefit or an accepted surrogate endpoint from a Tau biomarker result.

## Revisit
Review when a source is superseded, intended population/modality changes, or a real development stage is considered.
