# ADR-001 — Tau-first research scope

**Status: proposed** — awaiting owner review · type: `research-scope`

## Context
The supplied skeleton prioritizes Tau, but its candidate observations are fictional. A narrow scope supports a coherent review example; human trial counterevidence and interacting pathways prevent a claim that Tau-first is clinically validated.

## Decision
Propose Tau aggregation/seeding small molecules as the initial demonstration domain. Keep amyloid and inflammation as comparator evidence. Treat this as a reviewable research-scope choice, not a proven therapeutic strategy or a software architecture decision.

## Alternatives
| Option | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Amyloid-first | Stronger comparator trial context, but changes the supplied demonstration premise. |
| Multiple pathways at once | Wider exploration, but less comparable candidates and more retrieval/context cost. |

## Consequences
A focused dataset makes review behavior easier to evaluate. It also risks anchoring on a favored mechanism; negative and discordant Tau trials must remain in the evidence set.

## Validation
Review the strategy against papers 010–013 and distinguish antibody, ASO, and small-molecule mechanisms. No candidate result is established by a background citation.

## Revisit
Reconsider if the user changes the research scope, orthogonal evidence undermines the mechanism, or a different pathway better explains the observations.
