# ADR-005 — Evidence-backed candidate ratings with explicit uncertainty

**Status: accepted** 2026-11-09 — accepted by the project owner before implementation · type: `decision-model`

## Context
The supplied weights are illustrative, and different assay dimensions cannot be interpreted as a validated success probability. Missing or disputed evidence must not appear as a reliable current rating.

## Decision
Retain the 40/30/20/10 demonstration weights and higher-is-better scale. Leave a current rating unknown when evidence is missing or materially disputed; preserve earlier scores separately as history. Treat manufacturing ratings as preliminary developability proxies, not qualified CMC evidence.

## Alternatives
| Option | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Impute missing values | Produces a complete ranking at the cost of hiding uncertainty. |
| Rank only by potency | Simple, but ignores exposure, safety, reproducibility, and developability. |

## Consequences
Current totals remain interpretable and historical mistakes remain visible. More candidates may be unrankable, and qualitative anchors require human judgment. Neither weights nor thresholds are empirically validated.

## Validation
Existing code verifies arithmetic and null totals. Rating evidence is documented in candidate metadata; scientific relevance and the new evidence annotations still require human review.

## Revisit
Revisit after real study endpoints and decision outcomes support calibration. Do not silently change weights or recompute old decisions without recording the basis.
