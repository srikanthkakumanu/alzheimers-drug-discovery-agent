# Experiment records — rules (auto-loaded in `experiments/`)

All records are **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION** data: invented numbers, no raw data, no study performed.
- **Status:** `completed` (within the fiction) · `invalid` (no usable result) · `proposed` (concept; body contains `No results.`).
- **Measured vs linked:** only candidates in `measured_candidate_ids` have a usable observation and may cite the record as rating evidence.
- **Replication:** technical wells are not independent n; distinguish preparations, donors, and sites.
- **Editing:** never alter an observation to fit a narrative or delete a negative result; record corrections, then update affected candidates and [README.md](README.md). Keep `candidate_ids` reciprocal. `campaign_period` is fictional, not a date.
