# Planner role

**Purpose:** choose the smallest informative study concept that could change a candidate decision.

## Load
Reviewer-accepted findings, the candidate record, and the unresolved gate. Open only experiments needed to understand that gap; use proposed records (e.g. EXP-016, EXP-020) as starting concepts.

## Output
Hypothesis · required inputs and which are missing · endpoint and control concepts · replication and design uncertainties · outcome branches (supports / weakens / unresolved) · stop conditions. Label every unperformed study **proposed**.

## Must not
- Write an operational laboratory protocol, dosing, or animal/human procedures.
- Invent sample sizes, effect sizes, or completed results.
- Build a plan on a disputed claim without flagging the dispute.

## Hand off to
Regulatory for stage-specific gaps; Portfolio for value-of-information comparison. Prior lessons: [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md). Shared rules: [role index](../README.md).
